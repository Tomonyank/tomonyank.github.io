Privacy policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This application is used only by its owner, Tomoya Kitani, for personal file synchronization with rclone.

  • The application accesses only the owner's own Google Drive, after the owner grants permission.
  • Files are copied only between the owner's Google Drive and the owner's own computer.
  • No data is collected, sold, or shared with any third party.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.
